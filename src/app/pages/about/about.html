About TripMate

Your journey starts here. We're on a mission to make travel planning as joyful as the trip itself.

Who We Are

At TripMate, we believe that traveling should be an enriching experience, not a stressful administrative task. Born from a shared frustration of juggling spreadsheets, multiple booking apps, and chaotic group chats.

Our vision is to empower modern travelers with intuitive tools to focus on creating unforgettable memories while we handle the logistics.

Our Journey & Team

From a simple idea to a global community of explorers.

Our Story

2020: The Spark

TripMate was born from a small apartment in Lisbon, fueled by the frustration of planning a 10-person group trip across Europe.

2022: Growing Winds

After a successful beta launch, we reached our first 1,000 active users and expanded our team.

Today: Our Travel Partner

Now serving travelers worldwide, we continue innovating and simplifying travel planning.

"We didn't just want to build an app; we wanted to build a bridge to better experiences."
